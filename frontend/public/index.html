FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions you're probably thinking

Does it replace my sales team? +

No. DealForge handles initial qualification and negotiation within your defined guardrails. It escalates to human reps for exceptional cases, complex objections, or any situation outside its policy boundaries. Think of it as a tireless SDR that never goes off-script.

Can the AI give unauthorized discounts? +

Impossible by design. The 3-tier policy engine enforces hard limits. The AI literally cannot bypass the policy — it's not a prompt, it's deterministic code.

How does the human approval flow work? +

When the AI needs approval, it says "Let me check with my manager". An approval card appears on the manager's dashboard in real-time. The manager clicks Approve or Reject. On the next customer turn, the AI picks up the decision and responds accordingly.
